# Valheim Heraldry Mod

Write a coat of arms in heraldic language, or pick it from dropdowns, and see it painted on banners, shields
and tabards. A coat of arms is stored as its blazon (a line of text such as *"Azure, a bend or"*), and every
player draws the arms from that text. No images are sent between players, except the charge pictures that
players add themselves.

## Features
- **Heraldic banners**: a hanging banner, a tall free-standing large standard, and three hanging cloths (a
  curtain, a narrow curtain and a wide drape). Walk up, press **E** and write your arms.
- **Painted shields**: paint any vanilla shield at the **herald's table**. The arms show in your hand, on your
  back, on the ground, on item and armour stands, and on the inventory icon. Quality and enchantments are kept.
- **Heraldic tabard**: crafted and painted at the herald's table. The front carries the full arms; the back
  repeats the field and ordinaries without the charges.
- **Basic mode** for beginners: dropdowns for field, ordinary, charge and placement, with colours named
  plainly ("Red (gules)"). It writes the blazon for you, so you learn the language as you go.
- **Advanced mode**: write the blazon yourself, with a live preview. Wrong words get a red wavy underline,
  words that are understood but not proper blazon ("gold" instead of "or") get a yellow dashed one, and
  colour words get swatches. Hover over a word to see why. Save favourite arms and load them on any server.
- **Charges** (the pictures on a coat of arms): geometric charges drawn by the mod, the game's own trophy
  heads and any in-game item, and **your own pictures**, which are passed to other players on the server
  when they agree to take them.
- **Tartan**: weave a tartan from a traditional threadcount and use it like any colour, on banners, shields
  and tabards.
- **Mottos**: scrolls and ordinaries can carry words, in Latin letters or in runes.
- **Tattered**: put *slightly tattered*, *tattered* or *badly tattered* at the start of a blazon. Banners
  and tabards tear; shields get worn paint. The same arms always tear the same way.
- **Paint**: painting costs paint, mixed from berries, dandelions, coal and bone at the herald's table. Admins can
  configure everything, including switching paint costs off.
- **Other modders** can make their own shields and build pieces paintable by tagging a face in their model.
  No code is needed (see *For modders*).

## Getting started
1. Build the **herald's table** with the hammer, under the **Heraldry** tab: 10 wood and 6 leather scraps,
   near a workbench. It needs a roof.
2. At the table, **E** opens crafting (paints and the tabard), and **Shift+E** paints a shield or tabard
   from your inventory.
3. Build a **heraldic banner**, **large standard**, **curtain** or **drape** from the Heraldry tab and press
   **E** on it.
4. New to heraldry? Stay on the **Basic** tab. Each choice shows its blazon underneath, and you can carry it
   over to **Advanced** to keep editing. The **compendium** has a heraldry guide too: eight pages on writing a
   blazon, with examples.

## Writing a blazon
A blazon names the **field** first, then what lies on it. Capitals and commas are optional. The **Advanced**
tab is where you write it yourself, with the preview and the underlines guiding you.

[![Writing a blazon in Advanced mode](https://img.youtube.com/vi/2uveUN_iG6I/hqdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/2uveUN_iG6I)

*▶ Writing a blazon in Advanced mode (YouTube)*


| Blazon | What you get |
|---|---|
| `Azure, a bend or` | blue, a gold diagonal band |
| `Per pale gules and argent, a chief sable` | split red and silver, a black band across the top |
| `Quarterly or and azure, a bordure engrailed gules` | four quarters, a scalloped red border |
| `Barry of eight or and sable` | eight horizontal stripes |
| `Vert, a fess embattled argent between three mullets or` | a battlemented band between three stars |
| `Gules, three roundels in pale argent` | three discs in a column |
| `Gules, two stags' heads caboshed or` | the game's deer trophy, twice |
| `Sable, a "bronze axe" proper` | any game item by its name in quotes, in its own colours |
| `Azure, a scroll argent inscribed "FOR ODIN" sable` | a motto scroll |
| `Gules, a fess or inscribed "SKOL" in runes sable` | words in Elder Futhark |
| `Tattered gules, a saltire argent` | a battle-worn banner |
| `Tartan "B24 K4 B4 K4 B4 K20 G24 K6 G24 K20 B22 K4 B4", a bordure azure` | a woven tartan field |

**Tinctures**: or (gold), argent (silver), gules (red), azure (blue), sable (black), vert (green), purpure
(purple), tenné, sanguine, murrey; furs ermine, ermines, erminois, pean, vair, counter-vair, potent; any
colour as a hex code (`#2a6041`). Plain names (red, gold, ...) are understood but marked as not proper blazon.
**Fretty** (an interlaced trellis) goes on any of them: `Argent, fretty gules`, `Argent, a fess azure fretted or`.

**Field divisions**: per pale, per fess, per bend (sinister), per chevron, per saltire, quarterly; patterns
paly, barry, bendy, chequy, lozengy, gyronny (optionally "of eight" and so on).

**Ordinaries**: chief, fess, bar(s), pale, pallet(s), bend, bendlet(s), chevron, chevronel(s), cross, saltire,
pile, pall, bordure. **Lines**: engrailed, invected, indented, dancetty, wavy, nebuly, embattled.

**Geometric charges**: mullet (also "of six points", "pierced"), roundel (bezant, plate, torteau, hurt,
pellet, pomme, ogress, golpe), annulet, lozenge, mascle, rustre, fusil, billet, crescent, cross couped,
crosslet, pattée and potent, saltorel, scroll.

**Game charges**: heads (*a wolf's head*; also boar, stag, elk, bear, troll, lox, seal, serpent, goblin,
greydwarf, draugr, drake, asksvin), *a skull* or *a death's head*, the Forsaken by name (Eikthyr, the Elder,
Bonemass, Moder, Yagluth, the Queen, Fader), and **any item** by its in-game name in quotes: `a "wood" or`.

**Placement and style**: in chief, in base, in pale, in fess, in bend, in saltire, in orle; between; on a
(fess, chief, ...); points of the shield (*in dexter chief*, *in sinister base*, *in fess point*, ...);
*in the first and fourth quarters*; voided, fimbriated, counterchanged; contourné, inverted, reversed.

Good references: [drawshield.net](https://drawshield.net) and Wikipedia's "Glossary of heraldry".

## Tartan
*Tartan* isn't a blazon word; it's this mod's own addition. Write `tartan` followed by a traditional
**threadcount** in quotes, anywhere a colour can go: as the field (`Tartan "K4 R24 K24 Y4", a chief or`), on
an ordinary (`Or, a bend tartan "K4 R24 K24 Y4"`) or on a charge.

[![Making a tartan](https://img.youtube.com/vi/4RFmiBCh438/hqdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/4RFmiBCh438)

*▶ Making a tartan (YouTube)*

- Each stripe is a **colour code** followed by its **number of threads**: K black, W white, R red, B blue,
  G green, Y yellow, O orange, P purple, N grey, T brown, with L (light) or D (dark) in front (`DB`, `LG`).
- The count **mirrors** at its first and last stripes, as most tartans do. A slash marks a half-count pivot:
  `K/2 R24 K24 Y/2` is the same tartan as `K4 R24 K24 Y4`.
- A tartan that repeats without mirroring is written with `...` at both ends: `...K4 R24 K24 Y4...`.
- It's woven as a 2/2 twill and centred on the middle of the arms, so it sits the same way on every item.
- Each colour in the threadcount costs paint like a tincture.

**Looking for a real tartan?** The [Scottish Register of Tartans](https://www.tartanregister.gov.uk) lists
thousands of them with their threadcounts. Type the threadcount in quotes after `tartan`; if it uses a colour
code this mod doesn't know, the editor underlines it, and the nearest code from the list above will do.

## Paint
With paint costs on, each colour in the arms costs paint, split by how it is mixed. Red costs red paint; green
costs yellow and blue; white and black parts cost white and black. Painting the same arms again is free,
and so is removing arms. Paints are made at the herald's table, 4 bottles from each recipe:

| Paint | Default recipe |
|---|---|
| Red | 1 raspberry |
| Yellow | 1 dandelion |
| Blue | 1 blueberries |
| Black | 1 coal |
| White | 1 bone fragments |

Every recipe, every price and the paint used, along with the ceiling per painting, are server settings
(section `Paint`). `PaintCosts = false` makes painting free.

## Your own charges
After the first run you have the folder **Documents\ValheimHeraldryMod\Charges**.
- Put a PNG in it (`black_dog.png`) and use it in square brackets: `Or, a [black dog] sable`. The picture's
  shape becomes the charge. Give it a tincture to draw it in one colour, or *proper* to use its own colours.
- Optional mask, `black_dog.mask.png`, the same size: **red** = interior lines, **green** = claws and teeth
  (*armed*), **blue** = tongue (*langued*). Then `armed and langued gules` colours them.
- Packs: a folder or a .zip of PNGs, optionally with a `manifest.json` for several names, attitudes
  (`[wolf] rampant`) or atlases. Up to 1024 px per charge.

**On a server**, other players get your pictures from you through the server. Each player decides for
themselves:
- On joining, and whenever arms with a picture you don't have come into view, you're told so. Until then
  the charge shows as a magenta and black placeholder roundel.
- `heraldry_pending` lists the pictures you're missing, and `heraldry_get <name>` (or `heraldry_get all`)
  downloads them.
- `AutoGetServerCharges = true` downloads the server's own pictures (in its **ServerCharges** folder)
  without asking. Pictures from other players always need `heraldry_get`.

### Console commands (F5)
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `heraldry_pending` | pictures you've met but don't have |
| `heraldry_get <name>` / `all` | download them |
| `heraldry_remove <name>` | delete a downloaded picture (never your own files) |
| `heraldry_block` / `heraldry_unblock` / `heraldry_blocked` | never show a picture, for you only |
| `heraldry_info` | the arms you're looking at, with each picture's fingerprint |
| `heraldry_reload` | re-read your charge folder (admins: the server's too) |
| `heraldry_blacklist` / `heraldry_unblacklist` / `heraldry_blacklisted` | admin: ban a picture on the server for everyone |

Commands that take a name also work with no name on whatever you're looking at.

## Server settings
Server settings are synced to every client and only admins can change them. Some useful ones:
- `MaxBlazonLength` (500) and `MaxBlazonElements` (25).
- `MaxBannerResolution` (1024).
- `ShowMottos`: off hides the words on scrolls from everyone except admins.
- `MaxInscriptionLength` (40).
- `ExcludeShields`: shields that can't be painted (default `ShieldShare_`).
- `ExcludeGameIcons`: game items that can't be charges, or `all`.
- Charge size and upload limits.
- Section `Recipes`: build cost and crafting station of the heraldic banner, curtains and drape (defaults are the
  vanilla recipes).

Client settings include `BannerResolution`, `AutoGetServerCharges`, `ShareCharges` and the key used to edit
tagged pieces (`TaggedPieceEditModifier` + `TaggedPieceEditKey`, default Shift+E).

## Requirements and compatibility
- **Must be installed on the server and on every client, with the same version.**
- Works alongside **ShieldShare** (its shields are left alone by default), **TabardShare** (both tabards
  share one slot) and **BannerShare**.

## For modders
Make your shields or build pieces paintable without any code. In your model, name the paintable surface
object (or its material) **`ValheimHeraldryMod_Face`**. You can also add an empty named
**`ValheimHeraldryMod_CentrePoint`** to set the centre of the arms on a lopsided shape. Number the tags
(`ValheimHeraldryMod_Face_2` … `_4`) for up to four different patterns on one item, and add
**`ValheimHeraldryMod_UseUVs`** to use your own UVs on faces that wrap round. Without this mod installed, the tags
do nothing. The full spec is in
[ModderGuide.md](https://github.com/Hippoflotomas/Heraldry-Mod/blob/master/docs/ModderGuide.md).

## Known limitations
- Some charges are recognised but have no drawing of their own (lion, eagle, dragon, raven, hammer,
  fleur-de-lis, martlet, ...). They show the placeholder roundel. Use your own picture in brackets instead.
- Full marshalling (several coats of arms quartered into one) isn't supported.
- Controllers aren't supported in the blazon window.
